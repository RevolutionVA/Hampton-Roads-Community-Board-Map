Enter through the front door. The librarian will post flyers on the community bulletin board as long as there is a date and location listed.

VBPL staff email confirmation, reviewed October 13, 2026: all ten VBPL locations have community bulletin boards. Deliver flyers to each branch individually for staff review and approval. Letter-size (8.5 x 11 inches) is easiest to accommodate; tabloid-size flyers depend on available space. Include the event date and time, location, and organization contact information or website. Undated notices rotate out after about a month. Written posting instructions are recommended. Eligibility for nonprofit events that charge admission remains unanswered; confirm with staff before relying on acceptance. This email update does not establish a new in-person verification date. See [the staff-guidance summary in issue #19](https://github.com/RevolutionVA/Hampton-Roads-Community-Board-Map/issues/19).
