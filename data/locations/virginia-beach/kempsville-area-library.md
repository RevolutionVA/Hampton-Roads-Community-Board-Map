Enter through the front door. The librarian will post flyers on the community bulletin board as long as there is a date and location listed.

VBPL staff confirmed by email on October 13, 2026 that all ten library locations have community bulletin boards. Bring a flyer to each branch individually and leave it for review; staff post it if approved. No review turnaround time was specified. Restrictions are at each branch's discretion and depend on available space. Letter-size (8.5 x 11 inches) is easiest to accommodate; tabloid-size may fit when space allows. Include the event date and time, location, organization name, and relevant contact information or website. Undated flyers typically rotate off after about a month; this is approximate, not a guaranteed display period or a blanket 30-day limit for all flyers. A written note with posting preferences or special instructions is recommended. If a flyer is declined, you may request notification to retrieve it; otherwise staff may recycle it. Eligibility for nonprofit events that charge admission remains unanswered; confirm with staff before relying on acceptance. This email update does not establish a new in-person verification date. See [the staff-guidance summary in issue #19](https://github.com/RevolutionVA/Hampton-Roads-Community-Board-Map/issues/19).
