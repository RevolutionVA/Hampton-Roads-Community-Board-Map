VBPL staff email confirmation, reviewed October 13, 2026: all ten VBPL locations have community bulletin boards. Deliver flyers to each branch individually for staff review and approval. Letter-size (8.5 x 11 inches) is easiest to accommodate; tabloid-size flyers depend on available space. Include the event date and time, location, and organization contact information or website. Undated notices rotate out after about a month. Written posting instructions are recommended. Eligibility for nonprofit events that charge admission remains unanswered; confirm with staff before relying on acceptance.
