The Community Nook is a designated area for posting and distributing educational, civic, and cultural materials, with sections for City, Community, College, and Career items. Postings require library staff approval; items must be non-commercial, non-partisan, of local interest, advertise only free public events, and carry a discard-by date.

VBPL staff email confirmation, reviewed October 13, 2026: all ten VBPL locations have community bulletin boards. Deliver flyers to each branch individually for staff review and approval. Letter-size (8.5 x 11 inches) is easiest to accommodate; tabloid-size flyers depend on available space. Include the event date and time, location, and organization contact information or website. Undated notices rotate out after about a month. Written posting instructions are recommended. The Joint-Use Library's stricter requirements above still apply, including free public events only and a discard-by date; the general guidance about undated notices does not waive that requirement. The email did not answer whether paid-admission nonprofit events qualify elsewhere in the system. This email update does not establish a new in-person verification date. See [the staff-guidance summary in issue #19](https://github.com/RevolutionVA/Hampton-Roads-Community-Board-Map/issues/19).
